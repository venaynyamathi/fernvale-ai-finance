# Data dictionary

Generated by `python -m generator.build` from the live schema. Row counts reflect the current build.

## `data/source_systems.sqlite`: the operational systems

## Company & reference

### `company_info`  (12 rows)

Company facts and dataset dates (as-of date, books start, last closed month).

Columns: `key`, `value`

### `ref_plans`  (3 rows)

Price list by plan, before and after the January 2026 price increase.

Columns: `plan`, `segment`, `list_price`, `list_price_2026`

### `ref_departments`  (7 rows)

Departments and the P&L function each maps to (R&D, S&M, G&A, Cost of Revenue).

Columns: `department`, `pl_function`

## CRM (Salesforce-style)

### `crm_accounts`  (741 rows)

Every company sales has touched: prospects and customers.

Columns: `account_id`, `name`, `type`, `segment`, `industry`, `employee_count`, `account_owner_id`, `billing_customer_id`, `created_date`

### `crm_opportunities`  (653 rows)

New, expansion and renewal deals with stage, amount, seats, term, close date and the billing subscription each one became.

Columns: `opportunity_id`, `account_id`, `name`, `type`, `stage`, `probability`, `forecast_category`, `amount`, `services_amount`, `created_date`, `close_date`, `owner_id`, `contract_seats`, `contract_start_date`, `term_months`, `billing_subscription_id`, `lost_reason`

## Billing (Stripe Billing-style)

### `billing_customers`  (342 rows)

Paying customers, segment and payment method.

Columns: `customer_id`, `name`, `segment`, `industry`, `employee_count`, `state`, `payment_method`, `billing_email`, `stripe_customer_id`, `created_at`

### `billing_subscriptions`  (342 rows)

One subscription per customer: plan, seats, price, discount, current term, status.

Columns: `subscription_id`, `customer_id`, `plan`, `segment`, `billing_interval`, `status`, `start_date`, `current_term_start`, `current_term_end`, `term_months`, `seats`, `unit_price`, `discount_pct`, `services_amount`, `canceled_at`, `cancel_reason`

### `billing_subscription_events`  (764 rows)

Every MRR movement: new, expansion, contraction, renewal, price change, churn. ARR and retention are computed from this table.

Columns: `event_id`, `subscription_id`, `customer_id`, `event_date`, `event_type`, `seats_before`, `seats_after`, `unit_price_before`, `unit_price_after`, `mrr_before`, `mrr_after`, `reason`

### `billing_invoices`  (2,180 rows)

Invoices with status, amounts paid / written off / due.

Columns: `invoice_id`, `invoice_number`, `customer_id`, `subscription_id`, `invoice_date`, `due_date`, `collection_method`, `status`, `total`, `amount_paid`, `amount_written_off`, `amount_due`, `paid_date`, `voided_date`

### `billing_invoice_lines`  (2,224 rows)

Invoice detail with service periods, the basis for the deferred revenue waterfall.

Columns: `invoice_id`, `line_no`, `line_type`, `description`, `quantity`, `unit_price`, `amount`, `service_start`, `service_end`

### `billing_payments`  (2,275 rows)

Payment attempts by card, ACH and wire, including failures.

Columns: `payment_id`, `invoice_id`, `customer_id`, `attempted_at`, `amount`, `method`, `status`, `failure_reason`, `stripe_charge_id`

### `billing_refunds`  (4 rows)

Refunds issued to customers.

Columns: `refund_id`, `invoice_id`, `customer_id`, `refund_date`, `amount`, `reason`

## Stripe (payments)

### `stripe_balance_transactions`  (1,960 rows)

Card charges, fees, refunds and disputes flowing through the Stripe balance.

Columns: `balance_txn_id`, `type`, `source_id`, `invoice_id`, `customer_id`, `created`, `available_on`, `amount`, `fee`, `net`, `payout_id`, `description`

### `stripe_payouts`  (698 rows)

Net payouts from Stripe to the operating bank account.

Columns: `payout_id`, `created`, `arrival_date`, `amount`, `status`, `txn_count`

## HRIS & payroll (Gusto-style)

### `hr_employees`  (85 rows)

Everyone who has worked at the company, with department, title, hire/termination dates and current salary.

Columns: `employee_id`, `full_name`, `department`, `title`, `employment_type`, `location`, `hire_date`, `termination_date`, `termination_reason`, `annual_salary`, `is_quota_carrying`

### `hr_compensation_history`  (182 rows)

Salary changes over time (hires, January merit increases, promotions).

Columns: `employee_id`, `effective_date`, `annual_salary`, `reason`

### `payroll_runs`  (116 rows)

Semi-monthly payroll runs plus bonus runs.

Columns: `run_id`, `run_type`, `pay_period_start`, `pay_period_end`, `pay_date`, `funding_debit_date`, `status`

### `payroll_items`  (3,314 rows)

Per-employee pay detail: base, commission, bonus, withholding, employer taxes.

Columns: `run_id`, `employee_id`, `department`, `base_pay`, `commission_pay`, `bonus_pay`, `gross_pay`, `employee_taxes_withheld`, `net_pay`, `employer_taxes`

### `sales_commissions`  (122 rows)

Commission earned per closed deal and the month it is paid.

Columns: `opp_id`, `employee_id`, `close_date`, `pay_month`, `amount`

## Spend (Ramp-style AP and cards)

### `spend_vendors`  (42 rows)

Vendors with category, default department, payment channel and terms.

Columns: `vendor_id`, `name`, `category`, `default_department`, `payment_channel`, `payment_terms_days`, `accrue_monthly`

### `spend_bills`  (324 rows)

Vendor bills in the AP system, with service periods and approval/payment status.

Columns: `bill_id`, `vendor_id`, `vendor_name`, `invoice_number`, `invoice_date`, `received_date`, `due_date`, `service_period_start`, `service_period_end`, `amount`, `category`, `department`, `status`, `paid_date`, `memo`

### `spend_card_transactions`  (2,392 rows)

Corporate card swipes, coded by category and department, with receipt flags.

Columns: `transaction_id`, `transaction_date`, `post_date`, `merchant`, `vendor_id`, `amount`, `category`, `department`, `cardholder_employee_id`, `memo`, `receipt_attached`, `statement_id`

### `spend_card_statements`  (59 rows)

Monthly card statements and their payment dates.

Columns: `statement_id`, `period_start`, `period_end`, `statement_total`, `payment_date`, `status`

## Bank

### `bank_accounts`  (2 rows)

Operating checking and treasury money-market accounts.

Columns: `account_id`, `name`, `bank`, `type`, `last4`

### `bank_transactions`  (1,644 rows)

Bank statement lines for both accounts with running balances.

Columns: `bank_txn_id`, `account_id`, `posted_date`, `amount`, `description`, `counterparty`, `reference`, `txn_type`, `running_balance`

## FY2026 board plan

### `plan_budget_fy2026`  (659 rows)

Board-approved FY2026 P&L budget by month, GL account and department class.

Columns: `month`, `account_number`, `class`, `amount`, `driver`

### `plan_headcount_fy2026`  (84 rows)

Planned headcount by department by month.

Columns: `month`, `department`, `planned_headcount`

### `plan_arr_fy2026`  (12 rows)

Planned net new ARR and ending ARR by month.

Columns: `month`, `plan_net_new_arr`, `plan_ending_arr`

### `plan_assumptions`  (15 rows)

The assumptions the budget was built on (December 2025).

Columns: `assumption`, `value`

## `build/books_as_booked.sqlite`: what gets loaded into QuickBooks

Opening balances at 2024-11-01, then every transaction through 2026-10-31. Closed through September 2026; October 2026 is open (no month-end entries yet).

### `accounts`  (48 rows)

Chart of accounts with the QuickBooks account type/subtype each account is created as.

Columns: `account_number`, `name`, `qbo_account_type`, `qbo_account_subtype`, `statement`

### `classes`  (8 rows)

QuickBooks classes (departments) used to tag P&L lines.

Columns: `class`

### `gl_entries`  (8,915 rows)

Journal entry headers: date, period, document type (Invoice, Bill, Payment, Journal Entry...), source reference.

Columns: `je_id`, `txn_date`, `period`, `doc_type`, `source_ref`, `memo`, `entry_kind`

### `gl_lines`  (20,738 rows)

Journal entry lines: account, debit, credit, class, customer, vendor.

Columns: `je_id`, `line_no`, `account_number`, `account_name`, `debit`, `credit`, `class`, `customer_id`, `vendor_id`, `description`
